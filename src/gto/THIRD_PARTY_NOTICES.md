## phevaluator (hand evaluator algorithm)

The perfect-hash 7-card hand evaluator in
`src/gto/postflop/eval-tables.ts` and `src/gto/postflop/hand-eval.ts` ports the
algorithm from [phevaluator](https://github.com/HenryRLee/PokerHandEvaluator)
by Henry Lee (Apache License 2.0), via the gto-poker-overlay port above.

The distilled postflop policy network was trained on the
[PokerBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/RZ412/PokerBench) dataset
(solver-labeled decisions); verify the dataset's license before commercial
redistribution.

## Strategy caveat

The strategy data is a precomputed 6-max, 100bb-baseline reference. It is
provided for study and in-game guidance only, not as exact Nash equilibrium.
The short-stack push/fold tables in `data/pushfold.ts` are authored
approximations of standard push/fold charts. If this guidance is shipped in a
commercial product, please verify the chart licensing with the original
publishers (Greenline Poker / Pekarstas-style packs) or replace the data with
ranges generated by your own offline solver.
